Closing timing on RTL
with generative AI, formally checked

Point it at a design and its timing constraints. It synthesizes the design, locates the module driving the critical path, and asks a model to rewrite just that module. Every rewrite is formally proven equivalent to what it replaces before it's ever accepted — never assumed, never approximated. The rewritten module is reintegrated, the whole design is re-measured, and the loop repeats on whatever bottleneck emerges next, until nothing more can be improved.
